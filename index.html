    How to Play

    1. Speak and scheme. Click Parley under a faction portrait to open dialogue. Pick a reply or compose your own; their trust shifts with your words.
    2. Act in shadows. Drag a covert token (💰 🗡️ 🕯️ 🦉) from the Codex onto a map dropzone or a faction portrait to execute it.
    3. Manage resources. Watch Political Capital, Public Perception, and Factional Trust dials on the right. Keep them from withering.
    4. Read Fortuna. The interlaced serpents show Virtù vs. Fortuna. The ticker whispers the current winds.
    5. Advance time. Click End Turn to progress events; or let time pass automatically every 18 seconds.
    6. Advisor aid. Toggle AI Advisor for suggestions in the log. Use Draft Directive to have the AI propose a policy.
    7. Scenes. Use Courtyard/Market/Council/War Room for ambiance; your covert actions and talks matter most.
    Tips:
    • High trust (>60) makes factions "Favorable" and opens opportunities; low trust (<40) makes them "Wary".
    • Bribes raise trust but cost capital. Sabotage helps Virtù but harms public and trust. Rumors sway the crowd. Spies raise stability.
    • Save with a wax seal; unseal to continue.
